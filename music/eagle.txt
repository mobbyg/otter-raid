# Otter Raid - eagle attack.  E phrygian, 150 bpm (5 frames per 16th).
# One bar sting, then a 4 bar loop that repeats until the game
# calls music_play again (normally MUS_RIVER when the eagle leaves).
# Voice 3 (expendable) only adds stabs and hats.

tune EAGLE
tempo 5

pat sting1
  @crash c4 @bass o2 e4. e4. |
pat sting2
  @brass o5 b8 a+8 a8 g+8 g2 |
pat sting3
  @arpdim o4 e4. e4. e4 |

# loop: phrygian ostinato with snare, written on E
pat ebass
  @bass o2 e16 e16 f16 e16 @snare c8 @bass e16 g16 e16 e16 f16 e16
  @snare c8 @bass b-16 a16 |
pat etrill
  @trill o5 b1 | b1 | o6 c1 | o5 b2 g2 |
pat estab
  @arpdim o4 r8 e8 @hat c8 @arpdim e8 r8 e8 @hat c8 @arpdim e8 |

v1: sting1 LOOP ebass ebass ebass+1 ebass
v2: sting2 LOOP etrill
v3: sting3 LOOP estab estab estab+1 estab
